# Cimmich community code of conduct

Cimmich contributors and testers are expected to be constructive, patient and
careful with other people's private archives.

- Discuss the work, not the worth of the person doing it.
- Welcome questions and explain unfamiliar project boundaries without mockery.
- Do not publish another person's photos, names, library facts, credentials or
  private correspondence.
- Do not pressure anyone to share a real archive to prove a defect; use
  synthetic or redacted reproduction material.
- Respect maintainer decisions about release scope, owner authority and the
  separation between Cimmich and Immich.
- Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualised attention and deliberate
  privacy violations are not accepted.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's contact route
or private advisory form. Maintainers may edit or remove material, close or
lock discussions, and restrict participation when needed to protect the
community or archive owners.
